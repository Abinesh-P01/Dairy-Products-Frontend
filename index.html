About Lumina

Bringing the farm to your table since 2020

At Lumina, we believe that everyone deserves access to pure, high-quality dairy products. Our journey started with a simple mission: to connect local farmers with consumers who value freshness and quality.


We work closely with certified dairy farms that follow ethical practices and maintain the highest standards of hygiene and quality. From creamy milk to rich butter, from fresh yogurt to aged cheese, every product in our catalog is carefully selected and delivered with care.
